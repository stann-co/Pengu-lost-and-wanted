an ominous anomalous entity, first seen in the shape of an obelisk, similar to the one from 2001 a spacey odyssey. It is an alien entity, that for it's own unknown purposes, appears and influences characters.

[[Pengu]] first meets it in [[The past]] after venturing out from his flock, in search for food, and adventure. And after starring curiously at it for a while, starts to smack it.

Doing so, makes it suddenly burst into action, and encases Pengu in ice, though from the players perspective, all we see is the screen flashing white.

After which he is teleported to a colorful snow level, more akin to classic sonic levels and platformers.

In reality, this entire colorful snow level, is nothing but a sort of simulation. A vr playground, created to entertain, and study Pengu whilst he's actually in deep stasis.

Near the end of this long level, you meet the embodiment of the obelisk, a female character, sipping tea under an umbrella. Through this simulation, it is able to more clearly manifest itself and communicate. And it embarks some predictions and guidance for the upcoming adventure. "There's not many like you, yknow. A penguin in this world. It's time to find home Pengu, it's time to wake up"

The level Pengu is in, begins to glitch and crumble around him, revealing itself to be a simulation. Before it all falls apart.
