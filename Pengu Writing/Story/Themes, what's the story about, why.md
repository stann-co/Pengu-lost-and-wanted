### Hope
We live in a world of strife and suffering, and it could be easy to become pessimistic and depressed, but while those emotions are completely justified, i think we need optimism and hope.

That's what it should be about at it's core. Hope, and optimism

### Greed
A theme i think about often is that a lot of modern society is ruined by greed, people like to say capitalism is the root of all evil, but i think of it merely as a system, and it's still the best one we've come up with. But within those bounds people are too greedy and ruin it for everyone else.

If a system allows greedy people to fuck over everyone else does that make it bad? Maybe it does, but then every other system is bad too. Unless you could come up with a system that at it's core doesn't enable greed, you could argue for communism, maybe in theory but in practice the people at the top facilitating it are themselves also too greedy.

If greed is inevitable no matter what we do, maybe the solution isn't laws or rules, but people. Imagine if you could turn a greedy man, who only cared about themselves into one, who values the people around them. It's probably impossible, but it's a nice idea to strive towards.

Probably no matter what we do, there will always be people who dont have compassion for others, maybe it's sociopathy, maybe something else. And for those maybe the solution is to show that it isn't profitable to fuck people over. Others compassion has to do double duty for the ones they lack. Not supporting greedy tactics.

### Compassion
Maybe that's the real theme of the game. Our compassion have to fill in the gaps left by others

### Aten
Aten was a victim of peoples lack of compassion, and as a result became angry at the world. And the solution, is to make up for all that compassion that was lost.
