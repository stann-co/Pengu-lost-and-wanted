Aten too, is somehow affected by, and entangled by [[The obelisk]] 
Through similar means as [[Pengu]] She got entangled by its power, and now resides outside normal reality, only peering in through the corners of reality.

And she hates it, and wants it to end. How exactly she came to be this way, and how far her powers stretch is unclear, but she's using the powers she has in an attempt to influence different characters to achieve her goals.

She wants to get a corporal body again, and enact revenge on the world. And since Pengu also made contact with the obelisk, she's able to influence him more than others. She wants him to cause chaos in the world and make discord, and influence in such a way to make a machine that can let her be free again.

## Origin
Aten was originally an umbrella twirling aristocrat, from the 18xx's Victorina steampunk era.
She was simply a middleclass shop owner. And she did well for herself at a time. 

But after misfortune struck the city, her business was failing. And worse things followed.
Others lack of compassion, lead her to the end, and she was unable to feed her family or even son. She was filled with rage and anger at the people who mistreated her, and wish she could do something about it. And suddenly one night, within her room, the obelisk stood there. And it's presence mesmerized her.

She was never seen again, but the competing shops in the district was set aflame that night.
