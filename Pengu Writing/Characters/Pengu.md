A cute and mostly clueless baby penguin, that is filled with love and curiosity, and also is a bit cheeky. And can make a bit of a ruckus on his way. But never with ill intent.

Pengu is the last Penguin in the world, all of the others for whatever reason went extinct, and so most characters are at first pretty confused about what kind of bird he even is.
