# Collision sensors
collisions are based upon sensors all around the player.

the sensors detect solid objects or tiles, and then  returns how much to move back or forward to be just on the surface
So it can both snap the player down to the floor, with slopes, and up from the floor if you are inside it.


# obj_trigger
is a solid object, that sensors on the player detect. And then execute its trigger function.
Passing in Side, and the player instance id as parameters.

Side, let's you check if you hit the trigger from the top, bottom, left or right.
Making it easy to do one way collisions or other effects

player instance id. So you know which object did the detection. If it was the player, or perhaps a different bullet object
